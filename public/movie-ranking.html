Top Movies of All Time According to Angela

My top 3 best movies.

Avengers: Eng Game

The fighting scene is cool. The visual is awesome. The story is great.

Project Hail Merry

Quick banter, clever problem-solving loops, and high-energy direction keep the narrative moving.

The Fast and The Furious: Tokyo Drift

Captures tight multi-story parking garages, neon-lit alleys, and underground Tokyo nightlife.
